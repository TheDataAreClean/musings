> _iT's MeMe timE_

> – Prompt from [Bangalore IndieWebClub](https://blr.indiewebclub.org/) meetup #31.

***

I have always been an angry person, and I leaned into that anger to force the changes I wanted — in my life and in the world around me. It worked in my favour, even if I didn’t always appreciate the process. Reaching for anger as my default way of handling things always left a bad taste in my mouth.

Over the years I’ve learned to manage it better, leaning more on communication to discuss, engage, and resolve. My therapist would be proud, even though I haven't quite figured out where the anger is supposed to go.

Protesting alongside a younger generation these past few weeks has been eye-opening. Seeing memes and joy become the primary form of resistance has genuinely humbled me, and shifted how I think about change.

Watching people use humour to strip power from their oppressors, refusing to let them define the narrative, has been incredible. Somewhere between reading the protest signs, pointing them out to each other, laughing, and stopping to click photos, I felt my internal compass shift. Resistance can be sharp without losing its warmth, and serious without becoming joyless.

Here is to less angry days. Fraands, dosti bani rahe..

Enjoy some good memes. :)
